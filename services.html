Stay at Maletsunyane Gardens Lodge

A guest house with mountain views, close to the falls.

Maletsunyane Gardens Lodge is a four-bedroom guest house in Ribaneng with private bathrooms, mountain views and free on-site parking. Rooms have a bath, shower, tea and coffee maker, dining table and wardrobe, and guests can unwind in the hot tub. Reception staff speak English.

The lodge is child friendly, has security on site, and scores 8.7 across six reviews. Details are supplied by our partner booking.com.
