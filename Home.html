Welcoming visitors to Maletsunyane-fall

Maletsunyane Falls is one of Africa’s most
breathtaking natural wonders,
plunging a massive 192
meters (630 feet)
in a single,
uninterrupted drop.
Located near the town of Semonkong
in Lesotho, this majestic waterfall cascades
over a dramatic basalt cliff into a spectacular,
mist-shrouded river gorge.The sheer force of
the plummeting water creates a perpetual
cloud of spray at its base, inspiring
the local Sesotho name Lephalo,
which translates to
'Place of Smoke'. As one of the highest
single-drop waterfalls on the continent,
Maletsunyane is not only
a powerful symbol
of Lesotho's rugged, untouched
beauty but also a world-renowned destination
for extreme adventure.
